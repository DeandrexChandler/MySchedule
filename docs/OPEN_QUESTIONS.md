# Open Questions

Answer these in my own words, before each segment starts. When one is answered, move the answer to [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) and delete it here.

## Segment 0: Scope and setup

*Answered 2026-10-06. See DECISIONS D8 through D19.*

1. **Sky theme vs. dark/light:** when the app changes with the time of day, does the sky color tint the dark or light theme, or replace it? Is "sky mode" its own setting next to dark / light / system?
2. How strong should the shift between times of day be: a subtle wash in the background, an accent color, a gradient at the top?
3. **Weather:** live weather needs internet. When offline, is falling back to plain time-of-day colors (using the last known weather) fine?
4. Should the app use my location (for real sunrise/sunset times and weather), or should I set my city once?
5. **Deadlines, in my face:** what should happen as a deadline gets closer, and once it's missed? Walk through the escalation I picture, from "heads up" to "you can't ignore this".
6. Should anything ever block me from using the rest of the app until I decide "do it" or "move it"?
7. Is an alarm-style alert (rings even on silent / Focus) OK for the worst cases, or too much?
8. Anything in the docs that's wrong, missing, or not how I'd say it?

## Segment 1: Calendar

1. How do my classes repeat (days, times), and how do I want to enter a semester: one class at a time, or something faster?
2. What happens at the end of a semester: do classes just stop, get archived, something else?
3. What's the difference, in my head, between a **due-date line** and a **start-by line**? How should each look?
4. Can a line have a time but no date, or a date but no time?
5. What views do I picture: 1-day, 2-day, 3-day, week, month? Which one do I live in?
6. Do I want my existing Apple Calendar events to show up here, or does this replace it?
7. How should blocks be colored: by class, by type, by something else?
8. Should the sky colors also show *on* the timeline itself, e.g. the day's background shifting from sunrise at the top to night at the bottom?

## Segment 2: Inbox and tasks

1. When I tap the middle button, what's the very first thing I want to see?
2. What kinds of things do I throw in the inbox: tasks, reminders, ideas, notes?
3. How does an inbox item become "real"? Do I schedule it, give it a due date, or something else?
4. What's the difference between a to-do and a reminder for me, if any?
5. What should a reminder notification let me do right from the lock screen?

## Segment 3: Planning loop

1. What time at night should the "plan tomorrow" nudge come? Same every day, or different on weekends?
2. What counts as "I planned tomorrow"? Opening the app? Adding N items? Pressing a "done planning" button? Something else?
3. What time should the morning follow-up come if I didn't plan?
4. Should the follow-up be gentle, blunt, funny? Escalate if I ignore it?
5. Should missed planning count as a tracked streak too?

## Segment 4: Trackers

1. Full list of what I want to track right now (gym, BJJ, protein shake, Bible reading, anything else?)
2. For each: is it a streak, a days-since, a weekly count, or more than one?
3. Weekly targets for each (e.g. gym 4×/week)?
4. When does my week start?
5. Should completing a calendar block (e.g. "BJJ") check off the tracker automatically?
6. Do rest days break a streak?
7. How do I want to see progress: numbers, grids, rings, charts?

## Segment 5: Smart defaults

1. Which activities do I most want it to learn first?
2. When it predicts a time, should it fill it in or just suggest it?
3. Should "lift", "gym", and "workout" all be the same thing?

## Segment 6: Gaps and filler tasks

1. What are my typical waking hours (weekdays vs. weekends)?
2. What's the shortest gap worth suggesting something for?
3. What are example filler tasks I'd actually do?
4. Do location or energy level matter for what's realistic?

## Segment 7: AI

1. What do I picture the AI actually doing day to day? A coach, a planner, a chat, a weekly review?
2. What does "realistically improve my productivity" look like for me?
3. How do I feel about my schedule data going to a cloud AI vs. staying on-device?
4. Should the AI ever change my schedule by itself, or only suggest?

## Segment 8: Extras

1. **Quotes:** What kind (motivational, Bible verses, stoic, mixed)? A button only, or also a daily notification or widget?
2. **Subscriptions:** What do I want to know: monthly total, upcoming renewals, reminders before trial ends?
3. **Gym log:** Free-form notes, or structured (exercise, sets, reps, weight)? BJJ notes too?
