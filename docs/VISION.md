# Vision

## The problem

My life is split across a calendar, a to-do app, reminders, habit trackers and notes. None of them:
- know about each other,
- hold me to planning tomorrow,
- learn my habits,
- or work the way I think.

## The idea

One app that holds my **schedule** (classes, gym, BJJ, deadlines), my **tasks** and my **habits**. It also:

- **Makes me plan.** It nudges me at night to plan tomorrow, notices if I didn't, and reminds me in the morning.
- **Learns my patterns.** "Gym" means about X minutes, usually around a certain time on certain days. It figures that out from my own history, without AI where possible.
- **Fills gaps.** When there's free time, it suggests realistic things I could do.
- **Tracks consistency.** Streaks, days since, and weekly counts for what matters to me.
- **Coaches, realistically.** An AI that helps me improve productivity without fantasy schedules.

## Principles

1. **Offline is the default, not a fallback.** Every feature except AI works with zero internet.
2. **No accounts, ever.** No login, profile or subscription.
3. **Rules first, AI second.** If something can be done with plain logic and my own data, it is. AI is an extra layer, never a dependency.
4. **Capture fast, organize later.** Getting something out of my head should take seconds.
5. **Realistic over ideal.** Suggestions should match how I actually live, not how a productivity guru lives.
6. **My data stays mine.** Stored on my device. Anything that leaves the device (AI calls, sync) is opt-in and obvious.
7. **Built in segments.** Each segment is usable on its own before the next starts.

## Not goals

- Multi-user, sharing or social features
- Monetization, analytics, ads
- Supporting every possible workflow; it fits *my* workflow
